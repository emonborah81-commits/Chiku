01 · WHY YOU?

Why you?

I don't know if I can give one perfect reason why you are so special to me.

Maybe it's because with you, I can be completely myself. I don't have to pretend to be okay, strong, or perfect.

I love the way you understand the little things about me, the way you make normal moments feel special, and simply the comfort I feel when I'm with you.

You're special to me not because you're perfect, but because you're you.

"Somehow, my heart feels safest and happiest with you."
02 · WHAT I FEEL

What I feel

Honestly, I don't always know how to express what I feel for you.

I just know that you matter to me a lot. Your happiness affects me. When you're upset, I genuinely worry about you. When something good happens to you, I feel happy too.

I don't want to love you only through big words. I want it to show in the small things— remembering what matters to you, listening when you need someone, respecting your feelings, and being there when things aren't perfect.

I don't know what the future will look like, but right now, I know one thing for sure: I genuinely love having you in my life.

04 · ONE THING I WANT YOU TO KNOW

One thing I want you to know…

There's one thing I really want you to know.

I may not always say it perfectly, and sometimes I may not even know how to show it, but you mean a lot to me.

I don't love you because everything between us is always perfect. I love you because even when things get difficult, I still care. I still want to understand you. I still want to fix things instead of simply walking away.

I don't expect you to be perfect for me, and I don't want to be someone you have to pretend around. I just want us to be honest with each other, respect each other, support each other, and keep choosing each other through the little things.

You have become an important part of my life, and I'm genuinely grateful for every laugh, every conversation, every stupid little moment, and every memory we've made.

I don't know how to put the exact amount of love I have for you into words.

But if there's one thing I hope you always remember— you are deeply loved, genuinely valued, and incredibly special to me. ❤️
— Always, for you
